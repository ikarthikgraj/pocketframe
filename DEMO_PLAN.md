# Five-Minute Demo and Pitch Plan

## 0:00 to 0:25 - Problem

"Pocket FM already has a title, cover, and short synopsis for every show. But creating a visual trailer for every title requires creative direction, narration, video generation, review, and editing. That does not scale evenly across a large catalog."

## 0:25 to 0:45 - Product

"PocketFrame turns those existing catalog assets into an emotion-directed, human-approved trailer production line."

## 0:45 to 1:20 - Analyze synopsis

- Open seeded hero project or paste the synopsis.
- Show character, environment, mood, and visual direction.
- Point out FROM SYNOPSIS and AI INFERRED labels.

Line:

"The system separates canon from creative inference, so nothing inferred enters production without review."

## 1:20 to 2:00 - Emotional narration

- Play a neutral two-second comparison sample.
- Play the directed version.
- Show scene emotion and performance prompt.
- Approve narration.

Line:

"We do not simply convert text to speech. We direct a performance, then measure the approved audio to plan the matching shot length."

## 2:00 to 2:45 - Shot plan

- Show 4 to 6 scenes.
- Show exact narration, target duration, character lock, environment lock, and video prompt.
- Submit one generation request only if reliable.

## 2:45 to 3:30 - Human approval and retry

- Open cached version 1 of one weak shot.
- Reject with a visible reason.
- Open cached version 2.
- Approve version 2.

Line:

"A failed shot never destroys the trailer. Every scene is independently versioned and replaceable."

## 3:30 to 4:20 - Final trailer

- Click Stitch Trailer.
- If the live render completes quickly, open it.
- Otherwise immediately open the pre-rendered final.
- Play without speaking over it.

## 4:20 to 4:45 - Catalog scale

- Return to project list showing 6 or 7 shows in different states.
- Show one actual production report with elapsed time and number of retries.

## 4:45 to 5:00 - Close

"Pocket FM already owns the stories. PocketFrame converts the metadata that already exists for every show into a controlled trailer-production workflow. Every great audio story deserves a visual reason to press Play."

## Judge question answers

### Why not use a generic video generator?

"A generic generator creates clips. PocketFrame manages source grounding, voice direction, duration planning, versioning, review, failure recovery, and final assembly."

### Is a synopsis enough?

"It is enough for a synopsis-grounded concept trailer, not an episode-accurate recap. The product makes that boundary explicit."

### How does it affect revenue?

"The first production experiment is a show-page A/B test, using Episode 1 play conversion as the primary metric. We are not claiming uplift until it is measured."

### Does it really finish in two minutes?

"The result is a trailer under two minutes. The operator spends under two minutes providing input; media generation completes asynchronously."

### How do you handle failed generation?

"Retry only that shot, restore a prior version, upload a replacement, or use the cached fallback."
